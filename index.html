MT5 Forex Sync is a personal desktop utility that reads market data from MetaTrader 5, creates research reports, and synchronizes a designated report with the owner's Google Drive account.

What it does

The utility summarizes currency breadth and technical reversal-watch information. Timestamp and data-quality checks help the owner identify missing or outdated market data.

Why Google access is requested

The owner authorizes access so the utility can locate, upload, and update its designated report in Google Drive. Google displays the requested permissions during authorization. The utility is for the owner's personal use.

This website is informational. It does not offer sign-in, collect files, display private reports, or hold authentication credentials. The utility does not place trades.

Privacy and support

The privacy policy explains data handling and owner controls. For application questions, use the developer support contact displayed on the application's Google consent screen.
