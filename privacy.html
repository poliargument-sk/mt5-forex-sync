Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy describes the personal MT5 Forex Sync utility and this informational website.

Data processed

The utility reads market prices, historical candles, symbols, and timestamps from the owner's local MetaTrader 5 terminal. It creates technical market-research reports, local history, and processing logs. Reports are intended to contain market information rather than passwords or authentication credentials.

Google access and purpose

The owner grants Google authorization so the utility can locate, upload, and update a designated report in the owner's Google Drive. Google shows the requested permissions during authorization. The utility is not intended to inspect unrelated personal documents. This website does not request Google access.

Storage and sharing

Reports and history are stored on the owner's computer and the designated report is synchronized to the owner's Google Drive. Authentication is handled locally by the synchronization software. Authentication credentials and report content are not published on this website.

The utility does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, profiling, or model training. Any separate sharing or analysis of reports is under the owner's direction and the selected service's policies.

The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and owner controls

The owner controls local reports, history, logs, and cloud reports. Historical reports are not automatically deleted. Google Drive may retain versions or deleted items under its own settings. The owner can delete local and cloud files and revoke future access through Google Account connections. Revocation does not automatically delete previously stored files.

Security

The synchronization tool uses Google's HTTPS APIs. The owner is responsible for protecting their computer, Google account, local reports, and authentication configuration. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

This website

This website has no contact form, application database, advertising, or site-added analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts these static pages. GitHub may record visitor information such as IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links are subject to their destination services' policies.

Contact and updates

For application or privacy questions, use the developer support contact displayed on this application's Google consent screen. This policy may be updated if data handling changes; the revision date is shown above.
